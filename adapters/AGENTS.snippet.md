## Design research (Mobbin)

When designing, improving or reviewing a screen, flow or component, or when I ask how other apps do something, load the `mobbin-design-research` skill before starting. It lives at `<skills folder>/mobbin-design-research/SKILL.md` (for example `.agents/skills/`, `.claude/skills/`, `.cursor/skills/`, `.gemini/skills/` or `.github/skills/`). Follow it: search Mobbin with a question, look at the images, cite each `mobbin_url`, and finish with the capture-and-compare loop. If the Mobbin tools are not available, ask me for links or screenshots instead of pretending to search.
